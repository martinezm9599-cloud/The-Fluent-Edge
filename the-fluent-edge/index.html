How it works

  • 01Read the Spanish phrase and say it out loud in English before checking the answer.
  • 02Show answer reveals the English translation so you can self-correct.
  • 03Mark Correct or I got it wrong. If you miss one, it comes back a little later — not right away.
  • 04Enter your name once to save progress, and use Save & continue later mid-exercise to pick up where you left off.
  • 05At the end you get a summary of everything that tripped you up, with an option to review only those.
